Expect the Best.

I am running for Grade 11 Student Council Representative because I want our ideas to be heard and taken seriously.

Why I'm Running

Leadership starts with listening. I want to listen to what our grade needs and apply those changes.

With my experience as Student Council President, I already understand how our system works. This experience has taught me how to organize, communicate, and turn feedback into results, which is why I am prepared to serve as Grade 11 Representative.

If you have an idea, concern, or suggestion, I want to hear it. I will bring your feedback to student council and follow up on what we can do about it.

What Matters to Me

Student Representation

I want everyone in Grade 11 to have a say. I will listen to what matters to you and bring it forward.

Communication

I will keep you updated and make it easy to share feedback, whether something is going well or needs to change.

Positive Change

If students have a good idea, I want to help turn it into something we can actually do.

Ideas I Want to Bring Forward

More Events

More chances for students to spend time together, meet new people, and build a stronger school community.

Food Fest

A school-wide event where students can share food, try something new, and spend time together.

Culture Day

A chance to celebrate the different cultures and backgrounds that make up our school.

Spirit Days

Fun spirit days that give everyone a reason to get involved and represent the school.

Playoff Pep Rallies

Recognizing and supporting teams that make the playoffs with pep rallies and the school spirit they deserve.

Club Representation

Making sure clubs have a voice, their work is recognized, and students know what opportunities are available.

Teacher Icebucket

A fun fundraiser where students can help choose teachers to take the icebucket challenge.

Structured Intramurals

More organized intramurals with clear schedules, teams, and opportunities for everyone to play.

Bake Sale Fundraiser

A bake sale that brings students together while raising money for school activities and causes.

Battle of the Bands

A chance for student musicians to perform, compete, and show off their talent.

Better ideas happen when people work together.
